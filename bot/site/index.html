01 — Briefing

What this server is

The rules

No team killing, no racism, no cheats. Mics are not required but listening is. Admins are on most evenings and will remove you without a debate.

How it plays

Hardcore settings: no HUD markers, no killcam, friendly fire on. Rounds run 45 minutes. Squads hold ground rather than chase kills, so pace is slower than public servers.

Who it's for

Adults in EU timezones who want the same twenty faces every week. Solo players are fine — you'll get put in a squad. New players are fine too.

Mods

Three light mods: ballistics, medical, and a sound pack. About 1.2 GB. The Discord has a one-click collection link and someone will help if it breaks.
