01 — Briefing

What this server is

The server

NAW #1 Warfrogs: 100 slots, no grey fog, always up. Kicks and bans go straight to the Discord with the reason, so what happens on the server is public.

How it plays

King of the Hill across Bakurani, Zestafona and Kavkazi. Three factions, Lonestar, Valkyra and Manticore, race to the score cap. Kills and cash count toward the monthly leaderboard below.

The rules

No cheating, no team killing, no slurs. Admins remove you without a debate. Everything else is fair game; play the objective and talk to your team.

Getting in

Copy the server ID above, open WARDOGS and join by ID. The Discord has events, the live leaderboards, /stats for your own numbers and the people you will be playing with.
